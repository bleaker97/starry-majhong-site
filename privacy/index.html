Starry Mahjong Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · App version 1.2.7 · 繁體中文

Starry Mahjong ("the app", "we") is a single-player mahjong game. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app never asks who you are. From version 1.2.7 it keeps an anonymous copy of your game progress on our server, and, unless you turn it off, shares anonymous hands to help balance the difficulty. We don't sell or share any of it.

What stays on your device

Your game is saved on your phone: stars, marbles, level, shop items, daily missions, settings, statistics, and a log of the hands you played. Deleting the app or using "Reset progress" in Settings removes it from the phone. What the server receives is below.

What we collect

The app never asks for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn't use an advertising ID. When your phone is online, it sends two things:

  • A progress copy. The app signs in with an anonymous account (a random id, shown in Settings > Manage data as your Player ID) and keeps a copy of your game progress with it: marbles, stars, level, packs, collected sets, items you own, your recent marble changes, hands and games played, your rules, difficulty and language choices, the app version and your platform (Android, iOS or web). We use it to check the game's economy, to fix problems you report, and to send you gifts.
  • Anonymous hands, when "Help balance the difficulty" is on (it is on unless you turn it off; you can turn it off in Settings > Manage data or when the first hand starts). For each finished hand: the tiles dealt, the moves, the result and score, your rules and difficulty, the app version and the day it was played. These carry no account id, Player ID or device id; hands from one launch of the app share a random id that changes each time the app starts. We use them to tune the computer players and the difficulty levels.

We don't sell this information, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

Internet use

The game, including its voices and sounds, is fully contained in the app and plays without a connection. The progress copy and hands are sent when a connection is available, through Google Firebase (Google Cloud, United States), which stores them for us under Firebase's privacy and security terms. Like any internet connection, the server sees your IP address while it answers; we don't store it with your data.

Deleting your data

Settings > Manage data > "Delete my server data" removes your progress copy, any unclaimed gifts and the anonymous account from the server, and gives you a new Player ID. Shared hands can't be deleted, because nothing in them links them to you. Turning off "Help balance the difficulty" stops new hands from being sent. To ask about your data, email us with your Player ID.

How long we keep it

The progress copy is kept, and carries over to later versions of the app, until you delete it. Anonymous hands are kept for as long as they help balance the game.

Backups

Settings has a backup feature that saves your progress as a file, or as text you can copy. You choose where it goes, for example your files or a message to yourself. We do not receive it.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive that information from Google beyond anonymous install statistics shown to developers in Play Console.

Simulated gambling

The game uses "stars" as a betting score and "marbles" as an in-game currency. They have no real-world value. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out in this version, and nothing in the app involves real-money gambling.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version collects other data, shows third-party ads or adds purchases, we will update this policy first. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions: trayerstudio.dev@gmail.com

Starry Mahjong 私隱政策

最後更新:2026 年 10 月 31 日 · 應用程式版本 1.2.7 · English

Starry Mahjong(「本應用程式」、「我們」)是一款單人麻雀遊戲。本頁說明本應用程式如何處理你的資料。簡單來說:本應用程式不會詢問你的身分。由 1.2.7 版起,它會在我們的伺服器保存一份匿名的遊戲進度副本;除非你關閉相關選項,它也會分享匿名牌局,協助平衡難度。我們不會出售或分享任何這些資料。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

你的遊戲儲存在你的手機上,包括星星、彈珠、等級、商店物品、每日任務、設定、統計資料及牌局記錄。刪除本應用程式或在「設定」中選擇「重設進度」即可從手機清除。伺服器收到的資料見下文。

我們收集的資料

本應用程式不會索取你的姓名、電郵、位置、聯絡人、相片、麥克風或相機,不含第三方廣告、分析或追蹤工具,也不使用廣告識別碼。手機連上網絡時,它會傳送兩種資料:

  • 進度副本。本應用程式以匿名帳戶登入(一個隨機代號,在「設定」>「管理資料」中顯示為你的玩家代號),並以此保存一份遊戲進度副本:彈珠、星星、等級、卡包、收集的套組、擁有的物品、最近的彈珠變動、已玩的牌局及場數、你選擇的規則、難度和語言、應用程式版本及平台(Android、iOS 或網頁)。我們用它來檢查遊戲經濟、修正你回報的問題,以及向你送出禮物。
  • 匿名牌局,在「協助平衡難度」開啟時傳送(除非你關閉,否則預設開啟;你可在「設定」>「管理資料」或第一局開始時關閉)。每局完結後:發牌、每一步、結果和得分、你的規則和難度、應用程式版本及遊玩日期。這些資料不含帳戶代號、玩家代號或裝置代號;同一次開啟應用程式的牌局共用一個隨機代號,每次重新開啟都會更換。我們用它來調整電腦玩家和各難度。

我們不會出售這些資料、與任何人分享,或用於廣告。

網絡使用

遊戲(包括語音和音效)已完整內建於應用程式中,無需網絡即可遊玩。進度副本和牌局會在有網絡時,透過 Google Firebase(Google Cloud,美國)傳送,並依 Firebase 的私隱及安全條款代我們保存。和任何網絡連線一樣,伺服器回應時會看到你的 IP 位址;我們不會把它和你的資料一併保存。

刪除你的資料

「設定」>「管理資料」>「刪除我的伺服器資料」會從伺服器刪除你的進度副本、未領取的禮物和匿名帳戶,並給你新的玩家代號。已分享的牌局無法刪除,因為其中沒有任何資料能連結到你。關閉「協助平衡難度」後不會再傳送新的牌局。如對你的資料有疑問,請附上你的玩家代號電郵給我們。

保存期限

進度副本會一直保存,並沿用到本應用程式之後的版本,直至你刪除它為止。匿名牌局會在有助平衡遊戲期間保存。

備份

「設定」中的備份功能可將你的進度儲存為檔案或可複製的文字。你可自行選擇存放位置,例如檔案或傳給自己的訊息。我們不會收到備份。

Google Play

若你從 Google Play 安裝本應用程式,Google 會處理下載,並可能依據 Google 私隱權政策收集資料。除了在 Play Console 向開發者顯示的匿名安裝統計外,我們不會從 Google 收到這些資料。

模擬賭博

遊戲使用「星星」作為下注分數,「彈珠」作為遊戲內貨幣。兩者沒有現實價值,在此版本中不能購買、出售或兌換現金,應用程式內也沒有任何真錢賭博。

兒童

本應用程式並非針對 13 歲以下兒童,我們也不會蓄意收集任何人的資料。

更改

若日後版本收集其他資料、顯示第三方廣告或加入購買功能,我們會先更新本政策。頂部日期顯示最後更新時間。
