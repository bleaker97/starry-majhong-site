Starry Mahjong Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · App version 1.2.6 · 繁體中文

Starry Mahjong ("the app", "we") is a single-player mahjong game. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, send or share your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your game progress is saved on your phone only: stars, marbles, level, shop items, daily missions, settings, statistics, and a log of the hands you played. We never receive any of it. Deleting the app or using "Reset" in Settings removes it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts or sign-in. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn't use an advertising ID.

Internet use

The game, including its voices and sounds, is fully contained in the app and plays without a connection. This version does not send gameplay data to any server.

Backups

Settings has a backup feature that saves your progress as a file, or as text you can copy. You choose where it goes, for example your files or a message to yourself. We do not receive it.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy. We don't receive that information from Google beyond anonymous install statistics shown to developers in Play Console.

Simulated gambling

The game uses "stars" as a betting score and "marbles" as an in-game currency. They have no real-world value. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out in this version, and nothing in the app involves real-money gambling.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version collects data, shows ads or adds purchases, we will update this policy and ask for your permission in the app before anything is collected. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions: trayerstudio.dev@gmail.com

Starry Mahjong 私隱政策

最後更新:2026 年 10 月 17 日 · 應用程式版本 1.2.6 · English

Starry Mahjong(「本應用程式」、「我們」)是一款單人麻雀遊戲。本頁說明本應用程式如何處理你的資料。簡單來說:本應用程式不會收集、傳送或分享你的個人資料。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

你的遊戲進度只儲存在你的手機上,包括星星、彈珠、等級、商店物品、每日任務、設定、統計資料及牌局記錄。我們不會收到其中任何資料。刪除本應用程式或在「設定」中選擇「重設」即可清除。

我們收集的資料

沒有。本應用程式沒有帳戶或登入功能,不會索取你的姓名、電郵、位置、聯絡人、相片、麥克風或相機。應用程式不含廣告、分析或追蹤工具,也不使用廣告識別碼。

網絡使用

遊戲(包括語音和音效)已完整內建於應用程式中,無需網絡即可遊玩。此版本不會把遊戲資料傳送到任何伺服器。

備份

「設定」中的備份功能可將你的進度儲存為檔案或可複製的文字。你可自行選擇存放位置,例如檔案或傳給自己的訊息。我們不會收到備份。

Google Play

若你從 Google Play 安裝本應用程式,Google 會處理下載,並可能依據 Google 私隱權政策收集資料。除了在 Play Console 向開發者顯示的匿名安裝統計外,我們不會從 Google 收到這些資料。

模擬賭博

遊戲使用「星星」作為下注分數,「彈珠」作為遊戲內貨幣。兩者沒有現實價值,在此版本中不能購買、出售或兌換現金,應用程式內也沒有任何真錢賭博。

兒童

本應用程式並非針對 13 歲以下兒童,我們也不會蓄意收集任何人的資料。

更改

若日後版本收集資料、顯示廣告或加入購買功能,我們會更新本政策,並在收集任何資料前於應用程式內徵求你的同意。頂部日期顯示最後更新時間。
